## About BuilderClan

BuilderClan is an **open technology community** where builders learn through building.

We bring together **students, professionals, educators, researchers, founders, and makers** to collaborate on meaningful technology across hardware and software.

Whether you're writing your first line of code, designing embedded hardware, contributing to open source, conducting research, or launching a startup, BuilderClan provides a welcoming place to grow alongside other builders.

# Our Philosophy

> Great products, careers, and communities are built from the foundation up.

BuilderClan believes that the best way to learn technology is by creating real projects together.

We value:

- Learning by Building
- Collaboration
- Curiosity
- Open Knowledge
- Long-term Thinking
- Supporting Every Builder


# BuilderClan on Campus

**BuilderClan on Campus (BCC)** is our official campus chapter initiative.

It empowers universities and colleges to create thriving builder communities through:

- Project-Based Learning
- Open Source
- Workshops
- Hackathons
- Research
- Innovation
- Leadership
